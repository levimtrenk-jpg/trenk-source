How does it work?

Three steps. No copying links, no sketchy websites.

  1. 1 🎬

    Watch a video

    On YouTube, Shorts, TikTok or Instagram – a reel, a post or a regular video.

  2. 2 👆

    Tap TRENK

    On your phone: Share → "Download with TRENK".
    On your computer: the round button next to the video.

  3. 3 ✨

    Pick a quality – done

    Video up to 4K, or MP3 for songs and podcasts. The file is waiting in your gallery.

📌

Tip: to always see TRENK first when you tap "Share" – long-press it in the share sheet → Pin.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. No subscription, no ads, no sign-up.

Where are the files saved?

On your phone: videos in the gallery (Downloader folder), songs in Music. On your computer: in Downloads\Videos.

Is it safe?

The download runs on your own device, with no server in the middle. We never ask for your details, and an Instagram login is only needed for stories.

Why does my phone warn me when installing?

Because the app doesn't come from Google Play. It's the standard warning for any app downloaded from a website – approve it once and you're done.

A download failed – what now?

You almost never need to do anything: the engine updates itself, and if a download fails it repairs itself and tries again. If it still fails, the video was probably removed or is private.

Do you collect data?

We count visits and installs anonymously – no cookies and no personal data. Full details in the privacy policy.

Download

📱 Android phone

Share a video from the app and pick a quality

Download the app (APK, ~60MB)
  1. Open the downloaded file. If your phone asks – allow installing from the browser ("unknown sources").
  2. In YouTube / TikTok / Instagram: Share → Download with TRENK → pick MP4 or MP3.
  3. Videos are saved in the gallery (Movies/Downloader), MP3s in Music.

Android 8 and up, for phones from recent years (arm64).

💻 Windows computer + Chrome

A download button right inside YouTube, TikTok and Instagram

Download for PC (ZIP)
  1. Click the downloaded ZIP → Extract all.
  2. Double-click Install.bat. If "Windows protected your PC" appears – More info → Run anyway. The installer downloads what it needs by itself (a few minutes).
  3. Chrome opens at the end: turn on Developer mode (top right) → Load unpacked → Ctrl+V → Select folder. The path is already copied.

Windows 10/11. Files are saved in Downloads\Videos. The engine starts with your computer (icon next to the clock).
