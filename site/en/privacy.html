TRENK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

TRENK (Universal Downloader) is a free video download tool: an Android app, a Chrome extension and a local engine for Windows. The principle is simple: downloads run on your own device, and we don't know what you downloaded.

What we collect

  • Anonymous website visit counts – via GoatCounter, an analytics service that uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. It counts the page viewed, clicks on the download or tip buttons, browser type and approximate country. GoatCounter sees your IP address to estimate the country and does not store it, per its privacy policy.
  • New-install notification – on the app's first launch, one short message is sent via ntfy: "new install · app version · Android version". Clicking a download button on the website sends at most one message per browser ("download from site: phone/PC"). These messages contain no name, device ID or other information about you.
  • App crash and error reports – via Sentry, so we can fix problems. A report contains the technical error details, device model, Android version and app version, and for a download error only the site name (e.g. youtube) – never the video link. No IP address, user ID or downloaded content is sent.

What we don't collect

  • Which videos you watched or downloaded, or their links.
  • Your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or any other personal data. There is no sign-up and no account.
  • Device IDs, advertising IDs or fingerprints.
  • Your files. They stay on your device only.

Cookies

The website uses no cookies at all. It only stores a small flag in your browser (localStorage) so the download notification isn't sent twice.

Instagram login

If you log in to Instagram inside the app (or you're logged in to Instagram in Chrome and use the extension), the login cookies are stored only on your device and used only to download from Instagram. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can log out any time in the app's settings.

Selling and sharing data

We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone, and there are no ads in the app or on the website. The anonymous data above is kept by the service providers listed and is used only to know how many people use the tool and to fix problems.

Children

The tool is not designed to collect data from anyone, including children, and never asks for personal details.
